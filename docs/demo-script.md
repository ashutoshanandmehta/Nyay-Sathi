# Five minute demo

1. Open `http://localhost:5173`. Point out there is no login and the privacy notice in the sidebar.
2. Type: “I bought a phone for ₹18,000 on 2 June. It arrived defective and the seller refused the promised return.” Show the persisted chat and Your case drawer.
3. Type: “Actually it was ₹8,000.” Show the corrected value, prior-value contradiction record, incremented case version, and any stale draft marker.
4. Upload a synthetic receipt or screenshot. Show extraction status and preview. Never use a real person’s sensitive document in a demo.
5. Ask: “What should I do next?” With a configured key, inspect the official sources panel. Without a key, show the clear configuration-required state rather than a canned answer.
6. Open Knowledge Base. Show the intake branch for “refund processed,” the invoice evidence limits, current e-Jagriti route inputs/exceptions, the selected judgment distinctions, and the NALSA non-promise warning. Point out that these are shared public materials, not this user's private evidence.
7. Ask for a polite seller email, edit the resulting draft, and explain that a changed material fact makes an older draft stale.
8. Switch to Hindi or Hinglish without starting a new case. Record a short synthetic audio message and edit the transcript before sending.
9. Click New chat while the first case remains open. Enter a separate non-delivery matter and switch between the two cases to demonstrate isolation.
10. Export DOCX, PDF, and JSON. Show the one-page overview, chronology, evidence index, disputed facts, legal-review issues, urgent matters, and lawyer questions. Explain that nothing is automatically sent, filed, or signed.
11. Delete the synthetic case and explain that local rows, evidence files, exports, and checkpoints are removed, while external provider retention is governed by that provider’s terms.
