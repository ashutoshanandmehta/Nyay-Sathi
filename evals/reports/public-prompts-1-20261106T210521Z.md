Actual response:
**Assessment**

Since the milk is reported to be past its expiry date, the cautious choice is not to consume it; keep it separate and discard it. If you already drank some and feel unwell, contact a healthcare professional.

The date wording matters: US FDA guidance says a “use by” date is generally a manufacturer’s quality recommendation, not a food-safety date. That guidance does not establish whether this particular milk is safe, or determine the meaning of its label under Indian rules; the label wording and how the milk was handled are unknown.
